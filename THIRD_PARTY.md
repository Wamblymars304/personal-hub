## Sound recordings

The field recordings in `public/ambience/` are listed with their author, license and source in
`public/ambience/credits.json`. The app shows that credit while a recording plays in Atmospheres → Drift. They are under CC BY-SA 4.0,
CC BY 3.0, CC0 or public domain. If you redistribute them, keep that credit file with them. The CC BY-SA recording's
edits (trimming, levelling) are under CC BY-SA 4.0 too.

## Loaded at runtime (not included)

- **Fonts**: Inter, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono and Instrument Serif from Google Fonts, under the SIL Open Font
  License.
- **Map tiles**: Esri (World Dark Gray), with OpenStreetMap contributors' data. Attribution is shown on the map.
- **Data lookups**: Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Open Library, plus the Spotify embed player. Each is used
  under its own terms. They're only contacted when you use the feature that needs them.
